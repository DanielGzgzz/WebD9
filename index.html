Advanced Bulldozer Manual

Movement: Arrow Keys. Differential steering spins tracks opposite directions.

Surgical Blade Control: Use < and > to raise/lower blade.
Tip: Tilted back acts as a bucket to carry dirt. Tilted forward drops it smoothly for precise stacking.

Ripper Shank: Use ; and ' to raise/lower the rear ripper claw.

Rope & Towing: Press C near a vehicle to tether it to the Shank.

Refuel Truck: Press F to call the Oshkosh. (Requires slow speed to hook up).

Garage: Press G to spend Elite Coffee on upgrades.
